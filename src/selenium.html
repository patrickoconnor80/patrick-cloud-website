Selenium

MineralFoundational
TL;DR: Trace mineral required for antioxidant enzymes and thyroid hormone metabolism; can support stress-response indirectly via redox and thyroid pathways, but excess is harmful.

Deficiency effects

Lower antioxidant enzyme activity; may worsen thyroid function in low-iodine settings; Keshan disease (a selenium-deficiency cardiomyopathy) in severe-deficiency regions.

Intake

Aim around the RDA unless medically indicated for more. Food-based sources need caution — Brazil nuts in particular carry wildly variable selenium content depending on soil, so "a handful of Brazil nuts" isn't a reliable dose.

Top foods

  • Brazil nuts (content varies widely — see caution above)
  • Tuna (canned)
  • Eggs
  • Whole wheat bread
  • Brown rice
