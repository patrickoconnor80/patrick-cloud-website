Date night

Six dishes that look and taste like a restaurant plate, and that you can pull off without stress. Practice once first, prep everything before she arrives, and make one thing ahead. The desserts and drinks finish the night.

  • Scallop Scampi Linguine: 25 minutes, and fun to watch you sear
  • Crispy-Skin Sea Bass: crackling skin on French butter sauce
  • Cast-Iron Ribeye: the steakhouse dinner, sliced at the table
  • Shrimp & Clams Fra Diavolo: a big shared skillet, served in the pan
  • Red Wine Braised Short Ribs: made the day before, so you're relaxed when she arrives
  • Reverse-Sear Tenderloin: for when you want to go big
