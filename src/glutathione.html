Glutathione

CompoundExperimental
TL;DR: Glutathione (GSH) is a primary intracellular antioxidant critical for redox balance and detoxification.

Functions

  • Neutralizes reactive oxygen species
  • Substrate for glutathione peroxidase reactions
  • Participates in phase II detoxification via glutathione-S-transferase (GST)

Synthesis

Built from glycine + cysteine + glutamate, with cysteine usually the rate-limiting substrate of the three. See Glycine / Collagen and Cysteine for the upstream detail.1

Mechanism

Redox cycling between reduced (GSH) and oxidized (GSSG) forms, driven by NADPH-dependent glutathione reductase — which is why B2 (riboflavin, via FAD) and adequate NADPH-generating capacity both matter for keeping the system running.1

Intake / dosing

No established optimal oral dose — studies vary widely by formulation (liposomal, reduced GSH, precursor-based) and endpoint, and oral bioavailability of glutathione itself is limited, which is why precursors (glycine, cysteine/NAC) are often emphasized instead of glutathione directly.

Deficiency effects

Low glutathione status shows up in oxidative stress states rather than as a classic dietary deficiency.
