Copper

MineralUseful
TL;DR: Trace mineral required for connective tissue cross-linking (lysyl oxidase), antioxidant enzymes, and iron metabolism; too little can impair collagen strength.

Role

Copper's best-established mechanistic role in this series is as the cofactor for lysyl oxidase, the enzyme that cross-links collagen fibers into stable covalent bonds during connective tissue synthesis — see Glycine / Collagen for the full pathway. It's also required for several antioxidant enzymes (notably superoxide dismutase) and for iron metabolism, where it helps mobilize stored iron for use.
