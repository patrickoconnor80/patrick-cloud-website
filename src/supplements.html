Supplements

Herbs and compounds evaluated one at a time, for whether they earn a place at all.

This is a different list from Nutrients. That index covers essential and near-essential nutrients — things the body requires or clearly benefits from at known intakes. This page is about optional additions: herbs and isolated compounds with interesting mechanism stories, graded honestly for how thin the human evidence usually still is, with practical notes on form, dose, and sourcing for the ones that seem worth taking.

Same evidence grading as the rest of the series: Strong / Moderate / Animal–in-vitro / Inferred / Contested / Speculative. Most entries here lean toward the weaker end of that scale — broad preclinical evidence with limited human trial data is the norm for this category, not the exception.

Notes on this draft

Eight entries so far, and evidence quality varies a lot between them — Black Seed and Chinese Skullcap lean on preclinical (animal/cell-study) evidence with human data limited to small trials on adjacent endpoints, Beet Juice has the strongest and most direct human trial support, Pomegranate is the odd case where the mechanism is better established than the clinical literature (which is largely industry-funded and subject to an adverse FTC ruling on its advertising), Orange Juice is the one entry written as an adjudication between two loud public positions rather than an evaluation from scratch, Citrus Peel is the clearest case on the index of a genuinely interesting mechanism with a dose gap wide enough that the human relevance is unresolved, Mastic Gum is the second adjudication entry, and the clearest case of a supplement with a genuine evidence base being promoted for the one indication where its evidence fails, and Molecular Hydrogen sits in between: real small human RCTs exist, but the citation list is dominated by two research groups and was sourced from a tablet retailer's own blog rather than an independent review. That spread is disclosed inline on each page rather than smoothed over.
