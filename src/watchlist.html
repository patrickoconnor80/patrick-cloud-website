VCX · Fundrise Innovation Fund

Venture NAV Play

A 29-position venture portfolio — Anthropic, OpenAI, Databricks, Anduril, SpaceX. Anthropic prices its IPO around Nov 18 at $70–78, roughly the $965B mark already in the $21.70 NAV, and VCX has slid to about NAV. The premium has gone; the question is whether ANT trades well above its range.

TSLA · Tesla

Autonomy & Humanoid Robots

The thesis has moved off cars. Q3 free cash flow of $1.53B beat by about $6B, but on capex pushed into Q4 and with operating income down 56% — while the Roadster flew on Oct 15. Merger talks with SpaceX are reportedly far enough along for a December announcement, and the stock rallied ~16% into Nov 12 while the Nasdaq fell.

SPCX · SpaceX

Starlink, Starship & Beyond

Best execution record on this list. Starlink cash flows today, Starship now in orbit carrying Starlink V3s, an ~$8B spectrum deal to turn Starlink into a phone carrier, and a Q3 with AI at more than half of $14.6B of revenue and a first $4.4B profit — alongside a $35–40B Q4 capex guide.

ONDS · Ondas Holdings

Drone Backlog vs. Dilution

Q3 revenue doubled to $167.5M (+64% organic), backlog hit $835M, and 2027 is guided to $1.1B+. But gross margin fell to 22% and the share count is 582M+. The question is whether backlog converts faster than the float grows, and now at what margin.

IONQ · IonQ

Superion & the Owned Fab

Q3 revenue of $174M (+336%) beat by $33M and the 2026 guide went to $505–515M, but most of the jump is the SkyWater fab, and adjusted losses nearly tripled. Tempo is shipping, the CEO says Superion 256 is sold out for 2027, and backlog is $1.2B. It touched ~$25 on Nov 5, ran to $34, and fell back to $28.88 when Nvidia lost its CEO. Superion goes on show at SC26 on Nov 17.

BFLY · Butterfly Network

Ultrasound-on-Chip as a Platform

Repositioning from probe maker to chip licensor. Midjourney, Aleph Neuro, Sam Altman's Merge Labs, and now Bridge Neurotech are all building on the same silicon — and Mendaera's Focalist is the first Embedded product on sale. Q3 beat again (revenue +52%, 73% gross margin) and guidance went up a third time, but the stock fell: two customers are each over 10% of revenue, and the "next Midjourney" is still unsigned.

PRME · Prime Medicine

Prime Editing, Short on Runway

The first human prime-editing data, a CGD BLA targeted for 1H 2027, the first Wilson patient dosed, and AATD cleared for the clinic. A $144M November raise brought pro forma cash to $210M and runway to the end of 2027.

DNA · Ginkgo Bioworks

Autonomous Labs for Biology AI

After $6B of losses, Ginkgo is selling its robot labs and the data they produce — Novo Nordisk, PNNL, Lilly TuneLab, and a new AbbVie/Takeda antibody consortium. The stock doubled in three weeks on partnerships alone, then swung between $18 and $11 in a single week. Q3 revenue was down only 6%, and the burn is falling.

NBIS · Nebius Group

The Best-Funded Neocloud

Yandex's founder rebuilt as a GPU cloud: Q3 revenue +571% to $981M and $6.4B ARR, but the stock fell 16% on a trimmed 2026 guide and $40–45B of 2027 capex. Includes a side-by-side with CoreWeave, IREN, and Oklo.
