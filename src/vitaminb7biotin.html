Vitamin B7 (Biotin)

VitaminFoundational
TL;DR: Vitamin B7 (biotin) is a cofactor for carboxylase enzymes involved in fat, glucose, and amino-acid metabolism.

Intake

Adequate intake commonly cited around 30 mcg/day for adults (varies by guideline).

Deficiency effects

Dermatitis, hair loss; conjunctivitis; neurologic symptoms (lethargy, paresthesias) in deficiency states — rare on a normal diet.
