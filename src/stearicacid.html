Stearic Acid (C18:0)

CompoundExperimental
TL;DR: Stearic acid is a saturated fatty acid found in animal fat and cocoa; it tends to have a more neutral effect on LDL compared with some other saturated fats when substituted calorie-for-calorie.

Functions

  • Energy source and membrane lipid component
  • Can be converted to oleic acid via SCD1 (stearoyl-CoA desaturase)

Excess effects

Context-dependent — high saturated fat intake patterns may raise LDL in some people, though stearic acid specifically is generally considered more neutral on that axis than other saturated fats (e.g., palmitic acid) when swapped calorie-for-calorie.

Deficiency effects

No known deficiency syndrome.

Top foods

  • Cocoa / dark chocolate (85% cacao)
  • Beef fat / tallow
  • Butter and dairy fat
  • Beef
  • Yogurt
