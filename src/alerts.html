Market Alerts

The Email Alerts

The models on the Data page also run as email alerts: a morning gamma map every trading day, heads-ups before scheduled events, pin setups when dealers are long gamma, and market-cycle signals at washouts, overheated readings and the turns out of each. This page lists when each one goes out and shows a sample of every email.

Schedule

All times Eastern. The gamma model runs every 15 minutes from 9:30am to 4:15pm on weekdays; the market-cycle model runs once each weekday at 5:30pm, after the day’s closes are in.

No repeats

Each alert fires once per setup. Pin alerts remember the ticker, strike and expiration already sent; event heads-ups go out in a single 15-minute slot; market-cycle alerts keep their state between runs, and the washout and overheated alerts re-arm only after the score returns inside ±40. Frequencies for the market-cycle alerts come from replaying the rules over 2019–2026; pin frequency is an estimate, since it depends on how often dealers sit long gamma.

High beta: trend break

When After the close, when SPHB first closes more than 2% under its 20-day EMA while its 15-day high is within 2% of its 52-week high. Re-arms at a new 52-week high.How often ~2 a year

The trailing stop for high-beta positions. It fires after the peak, usually 5–8% off the high, so it is not a top call; SPHB has recovered after most breaks. Its value is in the few large declines it sidestepped: early 2020, late 2018, February 2025 and October 2026.
