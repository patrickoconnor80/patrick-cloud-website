The Thesis in Short

Why the gap exists

Stale marks

The Q2 update (N-PORT filed Aug 28) lifted the official NAV from $18.97 to $21.70 as of June 30, but it still lags. One detail cuts against holders: VCX sold roughly a third of its Anthropic position during Q2 at the old price, right before marking the rest up. The model below still uses the audited March 31 figures as its base. Anthropic is carried at roughly a $350B pre-money mark — anchored to the February tranche VCX bought at cost — against a $900B Series H closed in May. Anduril, Databricks, and SpaceX have all repriced since the mark too.

Why it may persist

No forced catch-up

Fundrise updates NAV quarterly, so the reported number stays stale by construction between marks. That cuts both ways. At roughly $30 (early October, down from ~$34 in August) the market has already priced in a large share of the repricing, about a 38% premium to the $21.70 NAV. So the gap is an argument about magnitude, not direction. There's new supply too: since Sept 10, Fundrise can sell up to $1B of new VCX shares through an at-the-market program with Jefferies, at no less than NAV plus commissions. Selling above NAV adds to NAV per share, but it also caps how far the premium can run.
