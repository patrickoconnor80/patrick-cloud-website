§ 1

How These Are Written

Evidence grading

Every substantive claim carries an inline grade — Strong, Moderate, Animal/in-vitro, Inferred, Contested, or Speculative — attached to that specific claim rather than a whole section.

Sourcing disclosed

Where a synthesis originates from advocacy sources rather than a study demonstrating it end-to-end, that is stated at point of use, with mainstream reviews cited alongside.

Counterarguments

Each paper states the strongest case against its own thesis at full strength before answering it — and says plainly where the reply is weak.

Readability

A plain-language primer comes first, and enzyme-level detail is quarantined into optional blocks with a takeaway that carries the essential point.
