The Research

Mechanisms, studies and evidence grades for each claim above.

Core mechanism

Thiamine, as TPP, enables key dehydrogenase enzymes that convert carbohydrates into usable energy (ATP).

↑ back to summary

Energy metabolism

Required for pyruvate dehydrogenase (glycolysis → TCA cycle), α-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase (TCA cycle), and branched-chain α-ketoacid dehydrogenase.1 Without B1, glucose cannot be efficiently converted into ATP and energy production is impaired.

↑ back to summary

Nervous system

Supports neuronal energy metabolism and is required for proper nerve function and neurotransmission2 — the brain's heavy reliance on glucose makes demand for B1 correspondingly high.

↑ back to summary

Glucose / carbohydrate metabolism

Essential for converting glucose into acetyl-CoA, the entry point into the TCA cycle — links glycolysis to aerobic energy production.

↑ back to summary

Deficiency

Impaired energy metabolism, especially in brain and heart tissue,1 associated with fatigue, neurological dysfunction, and cardiovascular issues (beriberi).

↑ back to summary

Transport / absorption

Absorbed in the small intestine via active transport; transport can become saturated at high intake.
