Health Goals

Start from what you want to fix. Each guide ranks what to do, shows how it works, and links to the research.

The rest of the site is sorted by nutrient, food or paper. These pages are sorted by outcome: a short list ranked by how much each habit moves, one picture of the mechanism, and a few paragraphs on each lever. They're meant to read in about five minutes. The evidence, doses and references live on the entry and paper each guide links to, with the same grading as everywhere else.
