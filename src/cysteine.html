Cysteine

Amino AcidUseful
TL;DR: Sulfur-containing amino acid used to build glutathione (major cellular antioxidant) and support detox/antioxidant capacity.

Role

Cysteine is the sulfur-donating partner in glutathione synthesis (glycine + cysteine + glutamate → glutathione) and is generally considered the rate-limiting substrate in that pathway — see Glutathione and Glycine / Collagen for the fuller mechanism.

Intake

Dietary protein generally supplies sufficient cysteine. Where it's used deliberately (as N-acetylcysteine, NAC), dosing varies by goal and should be individualized rather than following a fixed target.

Deficiency effects

Low cysteine availability can impair glutathione synthesis and antioxidant capacity — typically seen in the context of broader malnutrition or illness rather than as an isolated deficiency.
