Iron

MineralFoundational
TL;DR: Essential mineral and enzyme cofactor; required for oxygen transport and for prolyl/lysyl hydroxylase activity in collagen synthesis (works with vitamin C to maintain Fe²⁺).

Role

Two roles dominate: oxygen transport (as the core of hemoglobin) and, mechanistically closer to the rest of this series, its function as the reduced Fe²⁺ cofactor that prolyl and lysyl hydroxylase need to stabilize the collagen triple helix. Vitamin C's job in that pathway is specifically to keep iron in this reduced, active form — see Vitamin C and Glycine / Collagen for the full mechanism.

This entry is thin in the source database — Deficiency Effects, Functions, and Optimal Intake are empty, and there's no body content or linked citations beyond the TL;DR. Given iron's central role in the collagen pathway already documented elsewhere, worth prioritizing for a full write-up.
