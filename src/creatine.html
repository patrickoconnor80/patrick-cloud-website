Creatine

CompoundUseful
TL;DR: Creatine supports rapid ATP regeneration via the phosphocreatine system, improving high-intensity performance capacity.

Functions

  • Buffers ATP during high-intensity effort
  • Supports repeated sprint/strength efforts

Mechanism

Phosphocreatine donates a phosphate group to ADP via creatine kinase, regenerating ATP faster than glycolysis or oxidative phosphorylation can — the reason it matters specifically for short, high-intensity bursts rather than endurance output. Synthesized in part from glycine + arginine, tying back to Glycine / Collagen's functional-use section.

Intake

Typical regimen: 3–5 g/day maintenance; optional loading protocol of 20 g/day split across doses for 5–7 days to saturate muscle stores faster.

Deficiency effects

No essential-nutrient deficiency syndrome, but low dietary intake (e.g., low meat/fish consumption) may reduce muscle creatine stores and performance capacity in some people.

Top foods

  • Beef
  • Pork
  • Chicken
  • Turkey
  • Salmon
  • Tuna
