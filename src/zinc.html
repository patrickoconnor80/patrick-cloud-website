Zinc

MineralFoundational
TL;DR: Zinc is an essential trace mineral supporting immune function, skin integrity, and enzyme activity.

Functions

  • Supports innate and adaptive immunity
  • Wound healing and tissue repair
  • Antioxidant enzyme support
  • Gene transcription via zinc finger proteins

Mechanism

Regulates immune cell signaling and cytokine responses; supports barrier tissues (skin and mucosa).

Intake

RDA 8 mg/day (women), 11 mg/day (men); upper limit 40 mg/day (adults). Absorption is reduced by high-phytate diets (whole grains, legumes eaten without preparation methods like soaking/sprouting that reduce phytate).

Deficiency effects

Impaired immune function, poor wound healing, hair loss, taste/smell changes; in severe deficiency, growth delay and skin lesions.

Cross-reference: zinc and copper

The Metabolic Health hub's supplement section notes that isolated high-dose zinc supplementation can drive copper deficiency over time — the two minerals compete for absorption. See Copper.
