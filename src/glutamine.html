Glutamine

Amino AcidUseful
TL;DR: Conditionally essential amino acid that fuels intestinal cells and supports gut barrier function — often used in "gut repair" protocols.

Role

Classified as conditionally essential: the body makes enough under normal conditions, but demand can exceed supply during illness, injury, or intense training, at which point dietary or supplemental glutamine becomes relevant. Its signature use case is as a primary fuel source for enterocytes (the cells lining the small intestine), which is the basis for its place in gut-repair protocols.

Intake

No RDA. Supplemental dosing varies by goal and is typically used short-term for targeted gut support rather than continuously.

Deficiency effects

In stress states (illness, major exertion, critical injury), low availability can impair gut and immune function — a clinical-context finding rather than something seen in ordinary diets.
